The problem

Cancellations quietly drain revenue

The slot isn't lost because patients don't want care. It's lost because winning it back takes phone tag, voicemails and manual calendar edits.

While staff work through the waitlist, the phones keep ringing, insurance cards need checking and forms need chasing. By the time someone says yes, the window has often closed.


Hiring more people doesn't fix this. A workflow built specifically for clinic front desks does.

Signature feature

Automatic waitlist calling

Typical scheduling tools store a waitlist and stop there. This one acts on it: it spots a cancellation, calls the best-fit patient, confirms the booking and updates the calendar, usually within a couple of minutes.

It doesn't send staff a task. It completes the task.
  • Detects cancellations as they happen
  • Ranks the waitlist by distance, urgency and fit
  • Places the call right away
  • Handles confirmation, rescheduling and intake
  • Writes back to your records system
